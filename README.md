## 🚀 Key Features

* **⚡ Interactive Electrical Load & Phase Calculator**: Real-time kW load estimator with single-phase vs three-phase balancing recommendations.
* **🔍 Breaker Tripping Diagnostic Tool**: Step-by-step interactive troubleshooter for residential breaker trips (ELCB, MCB, Phase imbalance).
* **📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Full offline service worker caching and installable on Android / iOS home screens.
* **🎯 Technical SEO & Google Rich Snippets**: Schema.org `LocalBusiness`, `Electrician`, and `Person` JSON-LD graphs with Open Graph & Twitter share cards.
* **🛡️ Security & Privacy Compliance**: Integrated privacy policy & ad disclosure modal compliant with Google AdSense and global privacy regulations.
* **💬 Instant Contact Channels**: Direct WhatsApp dispatch, phone dialer, and native contact form integration.
