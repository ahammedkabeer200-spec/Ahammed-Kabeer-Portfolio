Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Welcome to Ahammed Kabeer Technical Services & Portfolio (https://ahammed-kabeer-portfolio.vercel.app/). We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and confidence of our visitors. This policy outlines how we handle personal data, cookies, and advertising practices in full compliance with international standards and Google AdSense publisher policies.

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide to us:

  • Contact Forms & Direct Inquiries: When you submit a project inquiry or contact request via our form, we receive your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and project details solely to respond to your technical request and prepare quotations.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • You may also consult Google's official documentation on How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Web Analytics & Performance Metrics

We use standard performance analytics to ensure website speed, reliability, and security:

  • Vercel Speed Insights & Analytics: Monitors website load times, Core Web Vitals, and edge delivery performance without tracking personal identities.

4. Information Sharing & Protection
